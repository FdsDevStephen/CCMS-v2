BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE
================================================================================

The Petitioners belong to an agrarian family whose livelihood
depends entirely on the cultivation of agricultural land. The Petitioners
and their predecessors have been in continuous, peaceful possession
and cultivation of Survey No.157, measuring 2 Acres. situated at
Melckalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi —Taluk, Tumakuru
District, for over five decades. The Petitioners’ late father, Sri
Kalaiah S/o Beluraiah, having personally cultivated the land for
several decades, applied for grant of the said land under the socio-
beneficiary scheme for Depressed Classes implemented by the
Government of Karnataka.
Pursuant to a detailed enquiry, spot inspection, and reports from the
Village Accountant and Tahsildar, the Bagur Hukum Saguvali
Committee recommended the grant of 2 Acres in favour of Sn i
Kalaiah. Accordingly, the Tahsildar issued Official Memorandum
dated 22.11.2017 and subsequently, on 01.01.2018, issued Saguvali 5
Chit confirming the grant On 18.09.2024, the Tahsildar along with
survey officials conducted spot inspection and prepared a detailed
sketch demarcating the full extent of 2 Acres in possession of the
Petitioners.
The name of Sri Kalaiah was duly mutated in the revenue records and
RTC, with the Petitioners regularly paying land revenue taxes .
However, without issuing any prior notice or hearing, the Assistant
Director of Land Records (ADLR) unilaterally conducted Phodi and
Durasth operations and reduced the Petitioners’ land by 0.24 guntas,

BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE
================================================================================

The petitioner herein is in unauthorized occupation of 4 acres
of land in Sy.No.68/1 of Chikka Agrahara Village,
Bukkapatna Hobli, Sira Taluk, Tumkur District. The
petitioner herein sunk a bore-well in the land in question and
obtained electricity connection and planted Areca and
Banana plant. The petitioner filed application to seeking
regularization of the land in question for her unauthorized
occupation. The said application was not considered till date,
hence this Writ Petition.

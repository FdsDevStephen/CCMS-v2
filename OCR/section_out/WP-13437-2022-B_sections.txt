BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE
================================================================================

The petitioner was and has been in unauthorized
cultivation of 2 acres 15 guntas of land in Sy. No.245 of *
Halesoraba village, Soraba Taluk since the year 1970-71. The |
land in Sy. No.245 of Halesoraba belong to the Government and
set up all for the purpose of villagers as could be seen from RR-5
and RR-6. Upon inserting Section 94-B to the Karnataka Land
Revenue Act, 1964, paving way for regularization of
unauthorized cultivation of government lands, the petitioner
submitted application seeking regularization of his unauthorized *.
cultivation over 3 acres of land in Sy. No.245 within the time
allowed under law in Form No.53. Pursuant to the application,
the revenue authorities conducted measurement of his lands and
prepared sketch through mahazar and submitted the same to
the committee. The committee in turn on perusal of materials
placed before it by a unanimous resolution recommended to
reguiarize 2 acres 15 guntas of land in Sy. No.245 in favour of
the petitioner. Pursuant to the said recommendation, the
Tahasildhar passed a forma! order of grant, granting 2 acres 15
guntas of land in Sy. No.245 in favour of the petitioner in terms .
of Rule 108-D(3) of Karnataka Land Revenue Rules, 1966.
| Before that, the petitioner deposited premium amount as fixed
by the Tahasildhar in a sum of %4,052/- to the treasury. The
